Chromeset Support

Chromeset is a strength-training tracker for Apple Watch running watchOS 10 or later.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email tall.back@proton.me.

When reporting a problem, include your Apple Watch model, watchOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful, but remove any personal information you do not want to share. Please do not send passwords or private Health records.

Record a session

  1. Open Chromeset and tap Start session.
  2. Tap Finish set when you complete a set. The rest timer starts automatically.
  3. Use +30 sec to extend your rest, or Skip rest to continue sooner.
  4. When finished, open the session options and choose End session, then Save & end.
  5. View saved sessions in History.

Settings and rest alerts

In Settings, choose your rest duration, automatic exercise changes, haptics, sound, and appearance. If rest alerts are missing, check the app’s settings and notification permissions on your Apple devices. Device sound and Focus settings may affect alerts.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. Enable it in Chromeset’s Settings and grant the requested Health permissions to record strength workouts. If a workout is missing, check for a message in Chromeset, review Health permissions, and allow time for your Apple Watch and iPhone to sync.

Workout location

With location permission, Chromeset can save a session’s starting location. You can manage location permission in your device’s privacy settings. The app’s set and rest tracking works without location access.

Delete a session

Open the session in History and choose Delete session. This removes the session from Chromeset. A workout already saved to Apple Health remains there and must be deleted separately in Apple Health.

Privacy

Read the Chromeset Privacy Policy for details about local storage, Apple Health, location services, and support messages.
