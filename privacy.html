Chromeset Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Chromeset is an Apple Watch app developed by Kalle Kainu for recording strength-training sessions, sets, exercises, and rest periods. For privacy questions or support, contact tall.back@proton.me.

Data used by the app

Chromeset stores your session history, exercise and set records, timing information, and app preferences locally on your device. Session records can also include active calories and the workout’s starting location when the relevant permissions are granted. Chromeset does not require an account or upload these records to a server operated by the developer.

Apple Health

Apple Health integration is optional. With your permission, Chromeset accesses heart rate and active energy during a workout and saves strength-training workouts to Apple Health. When location permissions are also granted, the app can attach the workout’s starting location to the saved Health workout.

Health data is used to provide workout recording and summaries. It is not sold or used for advertising, marketing, or tracking. Apple manages Health storage and any synchronization or backup enabled through your Apple settings.

Location

With your permission, Chromeset requests a recent location near the start of a session, then stops location updates after obtaining a suitable location or timing out. It does not continuously record your route.

The app uses Apple’s geocoding service to turn that location into a readable place name. This lookup can send location coordinates to Apple. The resulting location and name are stored in your session history. Apple’s handling of its services is described in Apple’s privacy policy. Location is not sent to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and analytics

Chromeset contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking tools. Apple may separately process App Store transactions, diagnostics, or usage information according to your settings and Apple’s policies.

Your choices

You can use Chromeset’s session tracking without enabling Apple Health or granting location access. You can turn off Apple Health integration in Chromeset’s Settings and manage or revoke Health and location permissions through the relevant privacy settings on your Apple devices. Revoking permission prevents future access but does not remove previously saved records.

Retention and deletion

Session history remains in the app until you delete it. To remove a saved session, open it in History and choose Delete session. Deleting a session from Chromeset does not delete the corresponding Apple Health workout; manage and delete that data separately in Apple Health.

Removing the app removes its local app data. Copies may remain in device backups or Apple Health according to your Apple settings. The developer cannot access or remotely delete records held on your device or in your Apple Health account.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. These are processed through the support email provider, Proton, to respond to your request and retained only as needed to handle it or meet applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending sensitive workout or Health data unless necessary. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address above.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change, with the revision date shown above. For questions about this policy or your privacy rights, email tall.back@proton.me.
